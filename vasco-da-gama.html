The Chronicle

Origins of Obsession

Every phantom has a wound that made it so.

1471 A.D.

Born in Arkenza

A fog-covered port city where sailors' superstitions were the only religion. He was born between tides — his mother claimed the sea spoke during labor. No one believed her. Vasco did.

1488 A.D.

Shipwreck at Age 17

The merchant vessel Esperança fractured on an unmarked reef. Of 24 crew, three survived. Vasco floated on debris for nine days. He emerged not grateful — but deeply, permanently curious about what lay beyond the reef's edge.

1491 A.D.

Death of the Father

Admiral Henrique da Gama vanished on a classified expedition. His personal charts arrived home in a sealed chest — the contents were never made public. Vasco broke the seal at midnight. What he saw changed everything.

1494 A.D.

The Obsession Begins

He resigned his naval commission, sold his land, and purchased a decommissioned warship. He renamed it Aeternum Mare. Friends called it madness. He called it inheritance.

Psychological Profile

The Nature of the Man

01

Analytical & Strategic

Every conversation is a map. Every silence is a territory. He processes risk not through fear, but through architecture — building invisible frameworks before every decision.

⬡

02

Cold Under Pressure

When the storm reaches its peak, Vasco grows quieter — not calmer. His stillness is tactical. Crew members report that his composure in crisis is more unsettling than the crisis itself.

◈

03

Morally Ambiguous

He does not believe in good or evil as fixed coordinates. To him, morality is weather — useful information for navigation, but never a reason to anchor. The destination justifies the bearing.

⊗

04

Obsessive Explorer

He has mapped coastlines that don't appear on any official chart. He has named seventeen unnamed islands — none with human names. He says naming things after people is how they die twice.

Internal Cartography

The Unmapped Interior

He carries guilt like ballast — necessary weight to keep from rising too high.

Every voyage is also an act of repetition. He knows what happened to his father. He suspects he knows what will happen to him. He sails anyway — not because he is fearless, but because fear has become familiar.

The hidden route exists. He is certain of this. He is also certain that certainty has killed every man who sailed toward it before him.

He does not consider himself different. Only better prepared for the ending.
